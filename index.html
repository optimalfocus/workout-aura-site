Common Questions

Things people ask.

What is the best gym tracking app with gamification?

Workout Aura is a premium iOS gym tracker built around gamification. It has a 21-rank progression ladder, an XP economy with streak multipliers, 16 unlockable talismans, 21 auras, 26 mantras, and 16 multi-week structured campaigns. Most other gym trackers focus only on logging — Workout Aura adds long-form progression and identity customization.

What's a good alternative to Fitbod?

Workout Aura is a good Fitbod alternative if you prefer pre-designed periodized programs over AI-generated daily workouts. Fitbod auto-generates one workout at a time; Workout Aura ships 16 science-backed campaigns (2 to 12 weeks each) with phases, deloads, and periodization built in. Workout Aura is also more affordable at $39.99/year vs Fitbod's $79.99/year. See the full comparison →

What's a good alternative to Hevy?

Workout Aura is a good Hevy alternative if you prefer solo training over a social feed. Hevy is community-driven with follower feeds and shared workouts; Workout Aura is single-player, local-first, and built around intrinsic motivation through ranks and unlockables. Workout Aura also costs $39.99/year vs Hevy's $59.99/year. See the full comparison →

Which workout app has a ranking or leveling system like a video game?

Workout Aura is built around an RPG-style 21-rank ladder organized across six divisions (Initiate, Disciple, Hardened, Elite, Prestige, and Ascendant). Each workout earns XP with streak multipliers. Beyond ranks, lifters unlock 16 talismans, 21 auras, 7 XP ring styles, 9 rank stripes, and 26 mantras to customize their hero card.

Is Workout Aura worth it?

Workout Aura is worth it for lifters who want structured multi-week programs and progression-based motivation. At $39.99/year (about $3.33/month with a 7-day free trial), it's cheaper than Fitbod ($79.99/year) and Hevy ($59.99/year), and includes 16 campaigns, 413 exercises, 21 ranks, and the full 76+ cosmetic unlock system with no in-app paywalls. Lifters who only need basic set logging may prefer Strong at $29.99/year.

What devices does Workout Aura run on?

Workout Aura is built for iPhone (iOS 16+). It is iOS-only — no Android, no native iPad app, no web version. Optimized for the gym floor: works offline, doesn't drain battery, respects safe-area cutouts, keeps screen awake during workouts.

How is this different from Strong, Hevy, or Fitbod?

Strong is a clean barbell-focused logger. Hevy is community-driven and social. Fitbod auto-generates workouts via AI. Workout Aura is none of these — it treats training as a long-form practice with structured campaigns, a 21-rank progression ladder, talismans, auras, and an XP economy designed to keep you engaged across years.

Does Workout Aura work without internet?

Yes. Workout Aura is local-first and fully usable offline without an account. All training data is stored on your device by default. Cloud sync (via Sign in with Apple or Google) is optional and only used to back up across devices.

Can beginners use Workout Aura?

Yes. Workout Aura includes beginner-specific campaigns: Off The Couch (2-week starter), First Steps (4-week true beginner), and 5×5 The Proving Ground (12-week novice strength). Each includes phase explanations and form cues for 100+ exercises.

Do I need any equipment?

No. Bodyweight Blueprint, First Steps, and Off The Couch require no equipment. Most other campaigns assume access to a barbell, dumbbells, and a bench — but the exercise swap feature lets you substitute almost anything.

Is my data private?

Yes. By default all training data is stored locally on your device. Cloud sync is optional — sign in with Apple or Google to back up and sync across devices. No advertising IDs, no third-party analytics SDKs. We never sell your data and we never show ads. See our privacy policy.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The annual plan includes a 7-day free trial through the App Store so you can experience the full app before subscribing. Cancel anytime within the trial and you are not charged.

Can I import my existing workout history?

Not yet — but CSV and JSON export are built in for backups, and CSV import from common loggers is on the roadmap.

What if a campaign is too easy or too hard?

Every exercise can be swapped for a same-muscle alternative, and weights auto-suggest based on your previous sessions. If a phase isn't right, the periodization adjusts as you go.

Will I lose my data if I switch iPhones?

No. The easiest way is to sign in with Apple or Google in Settings → Account on both devices — your data syncs to the cloud automatically. Or use Settings → Export to save a CSV or JSON file before switching, then Settings → Import on your new device.
