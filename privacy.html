Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Workout Aura (“we”, “our”, “us”) handles information when you use the Workout Aura iOS application (the “App”). Your privacy matters to us — we built Workout Aura to be a private, on-device experience.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data You Enter

Workout Aura stores all training data you enter — exercises, sets, weights, reps, RPE, body measurements, notes, photos, and personal records — locally on your device using iOS local storage. We do not transmit this data to our servers. We do not have servers that store your training data.

1.2 Subscription Information

Subscription purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive only the information Apple provides to confirm your subscription status (e.g., active / expired). We do not receive your payment details, name, address, or email through the subscription system.

1.3 Diagnostic Data (Optional)

If you opt in to share diagnostic data with Apple, anonymized crash logs may be shared with us via Apple's standard developer channels. These contain no personal information and are used only to improve app stability.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other apps.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

3. How Your Data Is Used

All training data stays on your device and is used only to power your in-app experience: showing your history, calculating PRs, advancing campaigns, awarding XP, and generating progress charts. You can export your data at any time as a JSON file from the Settings screen.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, it is retained as long as you keep the App installed. You can permanently delete your data at any time from Settings → Reset, or by uninstalling the App. We have no ability to recover data once it is deleted from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Workout Aura is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us and we will assist with data deletion.

6. Third Parties

The App uses Apple-provided system services only (StoreKit for subscriptions, Haptics, optional HealthKit integration if you enable it). HealthKit data is read on-device and never transmitted. We use no third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not store personal data on our servers, GDPR / CCPA / similar requests are largely moot — you already have full control. You may at any time:

  • Export all your data from Settings → Export
  • Delete all your data from Settings → Reset
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will reflect any changes. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at support@myworkoutaura.com.
