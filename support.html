Common Issues

My XP / progress isn't showing up

Workout Aura stores all data locally on your device. If you've recently updated, force-quit and reopen the app. If your data appears missing, do not reset — contact us first so we can guide you through recovery.

I want to cancel my subscription

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find Workout Aura, and tap Cancel.

I'm switching iPhones

Use Settings → Export to save your training data as a JSON file before switching. On your new device, reinstall Workout Aura and use Settings → Import.

I want to suggest a new campaign

We love this. Send us your idea — what goal, what schedule, what your current pain point is. The best campaigns come from lifter feedback.

The app is crashing

Force-quit the app (swipe up in app switcher) and reopen. If the crash persists, please email us with your iPhone model and iOS version. We take crashes seriously.
